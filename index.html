How to read this profile

Thalweg bed: deepest DEM pixel on a 500 m perpendicular cross-section every 250 m along the centreline (echo-sounded bathymetry, PANGAEA DEM). DEM along SWOT centreline: DEM elevation sampled on the SWOT node centreline. SWOT WSE: node water-surface elevations from a high-flow event () and a low-flow event (); the shaded band is the water column at high flow. Gaps are unobserved sections of those passes. Dashed verticals mark the four gauges at their corrected chainages (0 km = Bingen).

About this dashboard

A digital twin of the Lower Rhine built for an MSc thesis on estimating river discharge from the SWOT satellite mission. Four PegelOnline gauging stations are the ground truth: Kaub, Bonn, Duisburg-Ruhrort and Wesel.

  • Water level: in-situ stage from WSV PegelOnline (15 min) against satellite altimetry water levels from DAHITI (DGFI-TUM).
  • Discharge: a modified Manning approach (Durand et al., 2023) driven by SWOT water-surface elevation, width and slope, with the unobserved channel area derived from echo-sounded bathymetry (PANGAEA DEM). Variants: fixed or SWOT-observed slope, literature or calibrated roughness. Error bars come from first-order uncertainty propagation.
  • Validation: SWOT discharge against same-day in-situ discharge with a 1:1 line and a least-squares fit, plus n, r, RMSE, bias, NSE and KGE.
  • Live layer: the most recent 30 days and the current measurement are fetched from the PegelOnline REST API on every page load. Station tiles show the current stage, its percentile within the 2023 to 2026 record, and the 24-hour trend.
  • Context models: GloFAS (Copernicus) and GEOGloWS reanalysis discharge on the discharge chart.

3D scene: CesiumJS with Esri World Imagery and Esri world elevation, a 2 m hillshade pyramid cut from an 84 GB DEM, and the thalweg and SWOT centreline. Author: Sharvil Upadhye. Source: github.com/Sharvil2410/Rhine-River-Monitoring.
